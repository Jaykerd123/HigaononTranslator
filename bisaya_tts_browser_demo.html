📝 About This Demo

🎯 Purpose: This demonstrates Bisaya (Cebuano) text-to-speech capability using your browser's built-in TTS.

🔊 Audio Quality: Browser TTS provides immediate audio feedback but may not have perfect Bisaya pronunciation. For high-quality Bisaya audio, use the Facebook MMS TTS model.

🌍 Language Support: The demo uses Filipino (Tagalog) TTS voices, which are the closest available to Bisaya in most browsers.

💡 Tips:

  • Use Chrome, Safari, or Edge for best TTS support
  • Click multiple times to replay phrases
  • Adjust your system volume as needed

🚀 For High-Quality Bisaya Audio:
